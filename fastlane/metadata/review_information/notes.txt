ProEstimate AI helps contractors organize renovation projects, create editable estimates, preview changes with AI, share client approvals and prepare invoices/PDF quotes.

REVIEW ACCOUNT
Sign in with the demo username and password supplied in the App Review login fields. The account uses a fresh workspace; it has no preloaded clients, projects, estimates or invoices. Create a project and client to explore the workflow. A project photo can be imported when trying an AI preview.

Pro features are enabled for this designated account through an administrative App Review grant. No subscription purchase is required to inspect these features. The grant is not an Apple purchase or receipt. Manual project creation and saved-work access stay free. AI requires an active Apple trial or subscription, except for this designated review grant. Normal Pro limits still apply: up to 20 AI previews, 20 AI-generated estimates and 50 PDF quote exports per rolling 30-day period, with daily/weekly limits as shown in the app.

Suggested review flow: sign in, open Home and tap New Project. Choose a work category and optional name, then add optional photos/notes. Basic manual projects can be saved without a photo or AI. The single AI toggle reveals AI-specific inputs; eligible Pro/trial users can generate previews and suggestions. Most projects have two input pages; paid lawn projects can add the measurement page. Open the saved project and tap Create estimate, or Review estimate on an existing estimate. Draft line items can be added, edited and removed; non-draft estimates are read-only. Review quantities, prices, markup and tax before using the separate PDF export, proposal sharing or invoice actions. AI previews and suggestions are optional starting points that users must review. The current primary image provider is fal.ai.

The app offers Pro monthly and annual auto-renewable subscriptions. Eligible new subscribers can start a three-day introductory trial on monthly or annual Pro, once per subscription group. Localized pricing and eligibility are shown by Apple before purchase. Restore Purchases is available in the app. Existing Premium purchases are supported for restoration; Premium is not offered as a new subscription.

Use the supplied email/password login for this review account. Apple and Google sign-in are alternative account-entry options. No third-party account is required for the demo login.
