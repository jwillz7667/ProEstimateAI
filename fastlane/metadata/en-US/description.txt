Snap a photo of any room. AI renders a photoreal remodel preview. Then it auto-prices every material and labor line so you have a fully-priced bid — branded, watermark-free, ready to send — in under a minute.

Works for solo handymen winning their next bid, contractors scaling client approvals, flippers scoping their next project, and homeowners pricing a kitchen before they pick up a phone.

— THE WORKFLOW —
1. Snap or import a before photo (skip the photo on lawn-care projects — pin the area on the satellite map instead)
2. AI renders a photoreal after-shot
3. Materials and labor populate at your chosen Standard, Premium, or Luxury tier
4. Send a branded PDF estimate, proposal, or invoice — or share a one-tap client approval link
